About the Show

Our 153rd Grafton Show,

bringing the valley together

2027 will see the running of the 153rd Grafton Show, open 9:00am to 9:00pm on Friday 16 and Saturday 17 April.

The Show brings the Clarence Valley community together, showcasing the region’s rural activities and interests, from equestrian events and farm displays to sideshows, food stalls and exhibitions. Don’t miss the fireworks for Saturday night’s show finale.
